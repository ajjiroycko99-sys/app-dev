# My Favorite Series

## 1. Lucifer
- **Director:** Sherwin Shilati, Nathan Hope  
- **Writer:** Tom Kapinos  
- **About:** A crime-solving fantasy series where Lucifer Morningstar, the Devil, lives in Los Angeles and helps the LAPD solve cases while dealing with celestial drama.

## 2. Prison Break
- **Director:** Bobby Roth, Kevin Hooks  
- **Writer:** Paul Scheuring  
- **About:** A thrilling story about two brothers — one wrongfully imprisoned, the other devising an elaborate plan to break him out.

## 3. Stranger Things
- **Director:** Shawn Levy, The Duffer Brothers  
- **Writer:** The Duffer Brothers  
- **About:** A sci-fi horror series set in the 1980s, revolving around a group of kids who encounter supernatural forces and secret government experiments.

## 4. Young Sheldon
- **Director:** Jaffar Mahmood, Alex Reid  
- **Writer:** Chuck Lorre, Steven Molaro  
- **About:** A comedy series that follows the childhood of Sheldon Cooper from *The Big Bang Theory* as he navigates life as a young genius in Texas.
